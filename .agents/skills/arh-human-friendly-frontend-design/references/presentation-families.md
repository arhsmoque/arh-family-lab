# Presentation Families

Presentation families express a coherent relationship among hierarchy, rhythm, typography, imagery, and color. They are not brands or complete themes.

## Calm domestic

Safe, warm, familiar, and trustworthy. Use warm neutral fields, restrained sage/earth accents, humanist text, moderate radii, documentary family/home imagery, and calm modular sections. Best for family, household, care, and home businesses. Avoid scrapbook clutter, pale meaningful text, childish roundness, and staged perfection.

## Quiet editorial

Mature, spacious, considered, and modern. Use strong typography, large but purposeful blocks, disciplined whitespace, image-led or split heroes, restrained accents, and clear reading measure. Best for education, wellbeing, family, local professional, and crafted products. Avoid oversized empty heroes, tiny luxury type, vague magazine copy, and content hidden below spectacle.

## Clear everyday

Direct, friendly, efficient, and unpretentious. Use familiar sans typography, strong labels, visible actions, balanced density, plain surfaces, and minimal decorative dependency. Best for community services, repeat tasks, household utilities, local commerce, and mixed-confidence audiences. Avoid admin-dashboard coldness, excessive gray, and utility without human warmth.

## Local crafted

Grounded, handmade, culturally connected, and distinctive. Derive limited accents or imagery from documented local material; use natural surfaces, human typography, and story only where it supports trust. Best for local commerce, food, makers, culture, and community. Avoid generic ethnic motifs, flags as decoration, texture behind body text, and untraceable cultural claims.

## Gentle wellbeing

Supportive, calm, humane, and non-clinical. Use low-noise hierarchy, stable navigation, soft decorative surfaces with strong text, modest motion, and generous recovery/opt-out. Best for wellbeing, learning support, family, and care. Avoid faintness, diagnostic palettes, forced positivity, and calming decoration that hides urgent help.

## Lively community

Welcoming, social, energetic, and locally relevant. Use clear rhythm, recognizable people/activity imagery, controlled high-energy accents, and visible participation actions. Best for events, youth/community programmes, casual local commerce, and neighbourhood initiatives. Avoid rainbow overload, flashing promotion, crowded badges, and novelty navigation.

## Compatibility rules

- Choose one primary family. Borrow at most one explicitly named trait from another family.
- Keep semantic safety tokens independent from decorative palette choices.
- Match density and motion to task consequence and attention context, not only desired style.
- Use a display face for identity and a highly readable body/utility face for facts and actions.
- Keep neutral surfaces dominant; use accent as punctuation and signal.
- Treat `premium` as restraint, material quality, hierarchy, and care—not black, gold, faint type, or exclusion.
